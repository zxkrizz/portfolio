How I work

01

Every change goes through a pipeline.

Infrastructure is defined in Terraform and shipped with GitHub Actions — planned, reviewed and applied the same way every time. No portal clicks that nobody can reproduce.

02

Golden paths, not gatekeeping.

I package the approved way of doing things into reusable modules and workflows, so teams get a secure, tagged, policy-compliant Azure environment by default rather than by review.

03

Built to heal itself.

Redundancy, health checks and automation come first; heroics come last. Try it — click any pod in the cluster and watch the platform replace it.

04

Measured, not assumed.

I have rolled out Zabbix, Grafana, Datadog and Wazuh across on-prem and cloud estates, and I alert on what users actually feel instead of on every CPU spike.

05

Accountable when it breaks.

I own business-critical environments in production. When something fails I fix it calmly, find the root cause, and automate it out of existence.
