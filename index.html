    ## approach
    
        
    
          
    01
    
          
    Everything is code, and code goes through a pipeline.
    
          
    Infrastructure lives in Terraform and ships with GitHub Actions — planned, reviewed and applied the same way every time. No portal clicks nobody can reproduce.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    02
    
          
    Golden paths, not gatekeeping.
    
          
    I package the approved way of doing things into reusable modules and workflows, so teams get a secure, tagged, policy-compliant environment by default.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    03
    
          
    Built to heal itself.
    
          
    Redundancy, health checks and automation first; heroics last. That goes for app pods and for GPU nodes alike. Try it — click any pod and watch the platform replace it.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    04
    
          
    Measured, not assumed.
    
          
    Grafana, Zabbix and Datadog across cloud, on-prem and GPU fleets — alerting on what users actually feel, plus GPU and interconnect health events.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    05
    
          
    Accountable when it breaks.
    
          
    When something fails I fix it calmly, find the root cause, and automate it out of existence.
